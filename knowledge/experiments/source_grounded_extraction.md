# Source-grounded extraction

## Hypothesis

Structured extraction with a source span can reduce manual intake effort without losing traceability.

## Scope

Use only text that the organization is allowed to process. Start with 30 representative, non-sensitive notes or documents.

## Compare

Manual source-card creation versus extraction-assisted source-card creation.

## Measures

- Factual accuracy after human review
- Correct source-span linkage
- Reviewer minutes per card
- Cost and operational dependency

## Exit rule

Do not adopt if source links are unreliable, manual correction exceeds the saved time, or data handling approval is absent.
